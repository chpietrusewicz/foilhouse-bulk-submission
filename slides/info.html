Foil House buys Pokémon cards in bulk from sellers across the U.S. You send us a submission, we review it, and we tell you if we want it and what we'd pay.

What we buy

Bulk commons and uncommons, holos and rares, binders and collections, sealed product, and graded cards. Sorted or unsorted is fine.

How it works

1. You submit the form with photos. 2. We review it and email you our decision. 3. If approved, you ship it from your post office and send us the receipt. 4. We reimburse your postage. 5. Once we've received and inspected the cards, we pay you.

Program policy

  • Approval isn't final. We approve based on your photos, and the final offer depends on what actually arrives.
  • If the lot doesn't match. If it's materially different from your photos, we may revise the offer. You can accept it or ask for the cards back.
  • Counterfeits and unsellable cards. We may remove fake or heavily damaged cards from the count.
  • Payment. We pay through PayPal within [X] business days of receiving your cards. Payment happens after inspection.
  • Postage. We reimburse USPS postage up to [$X] with a receipt and tracking number.
  • Ownership. You confirm the cards are yours and that you're 18 or older.
  • Your information. We use your details and photos only to review and complete your submission.
