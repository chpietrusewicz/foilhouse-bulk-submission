Only ship once we've approved your submission. We'll email you the address to send it to.

  1. Wait for approvalOur approval email has the ship-to address and the most we'll reimburse for postage.
  2. Pack it tightlyPut valuable cards in sleeves and toploaders, and bulk in a sturdy box with no room to shift. Tape it shut.
  3. Ship from the post officeUnless we say otherwise, ask for USPS Ground Advantage with tracking. Don't pay for extras like overnight shipping, since we won't reimburse them.
  4. Keep your receiptMake sure it shows the postage cost and the tracking number.
  5. Send us the receiptReply to the approval email with a clear photo of the receipt and your tracking number.
  6. Get paidWe reimburse your postage, and once your cards arrive and we've checked them, we pay you for the lot.
